Genre lists (crates/chilpa-library/data)

genres-known.txt is the list of genres of the MusicBrainz database
(https://musicbrainz.org/genres), taken on 2026-09-29. The genre table is
part of the database's core data, which is released under CC0.

genres-whole.txt lists genre names that catalogs publish with a separator
inside them. They are names, used only to tell a tag that states one of
them from a tag that states several genres.
