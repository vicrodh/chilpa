# 0.1.1 - Chilpa is alive!

This is the first release of Chilpa. As most of you know, it is the spiritual successor of QBZ, but with due distance. Instead of being focused on being THE CLIENT of one platform, Chilpa is built to make playing your high-quality music easy.

No feature here comes from trying to find a new angle or reinvent the wheel. Most of the tools already exist, and I took inspiration from my favorite players: Strawberry, Fooyin/foobar2000, MusicBee, MediaMonkey, and even the long-gone iTunes and MusicMatch. Chilpa has details from all of them without turning into any of them. Above all, I carried over much of what I learned, and the feedback I collected, from QBZ.

I expect a lot of feedback during this first release: issues and everything else from the community. My dream is that people create plugins for the application, share them with the community, and everyone can shape the application to their own needs.

I also have a personal roadmap for Chilpa. I want it to have its own daemon, agnostic to any streaming platform and working for all of them, and a mobile app that controls everything from there and lets you manage your DAP without pain.

With that, here is the first version of Chilpa. I am looking forward to your comments.
